# Release Notes

Version v0.3.4 — August 3, 2026

## Notification colors match the team bar

The parse notification used to color its counts on its own — allies always
green, enemies always red, in shades that didn't quite match anything. It
now shows the same per-team breakdown as the team bar, in the exact same
red/green/blue. If your team is blue, your number is blue in both places.

NOTE: A three-way fight now shows three numbers in the toast instead of the
old ally/enemy pair — that's what keeps it in lockstep with the bar.
